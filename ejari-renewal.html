What is Ejari Renewal & Cancellation?

Your Ejari certificate is only valid while the underlying tenancy is — and once it lapses, everything connected to it starts failing: trade license renewals get blocked, visa applications stall, DEWA and banking updates bounce. Ejari renewal is quick when handled correctly, and disproportionately painful when it isn't.

We renew every Ejari type — virtual, business center, office, shop, warehouse and labor camp — usually within 10–20 minutes. If you're moving premises or closing down, we also handle Ejari cancellation, which is a required step before the same unit can be registered to a new tenant and before certain license amendments.

Best of all: every renewal client goes into our free reminder system. We ping you on WhatsApp before your Ejari expires next year, so you never get caught by a blocked license renewal again.

Who Needs Ejari Renewal & Cancellation?

  • Companies renewing their DED trade license
  • Tenants extending their tenancy for another year
  • Businesses relocating to new premises (cancel + new Ejari)
  • Landlords needing an old Ejari cancelled
  • Companies fixing a lapsed/expired Ejari urgently

My Ejari expired and my license renewal is blocked — how fast can you fix it?
