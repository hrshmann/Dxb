1. What is Ejari?

Ejari (Arabic: إيجاري, meaning “my rent”) is Dubai's mandatory, government-run tenancy registration system. Introduced under Law No. 26 of 2007, which regulates the relationship between landlords and tenants in Dubai, it is operated by the Real Estate Regulatory Agency (RERA) under the Dubai Land Department (DLD).

Its purpose is simple and powerful: every rental contract in mainland Dubai — residential or commercial — is recorded in one official database. That single registration legalizes the tenancy, protects both parties, feeds Dubai's rental index, and acts as the key that unlocks nearly every government service tied to a property.

The output is the Ejari certificate: an official document carrying a unique registration number, instantly verifiable by anyone in the DLD's Dubai REST app. When a bank, DED, DEWA, immigration or a school asks for “your Ejari,” this certificate is what they mean.

Ejari is not paperwork for its own sake. It is the gateway document of life and business in Dubai:

🛂Residence & family visas

You cannot file a rental dispute case in Dubai without a registered Ejari — it is your legal standing as a tenant.

🏦Banking & compliance
Banks routinely request Ejari as premises proof when opening or reviewing corporate accounts.
📊RERA Rental Index

4. The Master Documents Checklist

For every registration

  • Signed tenancy contract (Dubai's unified contract format is Ejari-ready)
  • Landlord's title deed copy — the ownership proof issued by DLD
  • Tenant's passport copy, plus Emirates ID and visa page for residents
  • DEWA premise number of the unit (from a bill or the meter)

Additionally for commercial Ejari

  • Trade license copy — or the initial approval for brand-new setups
  • Authorized signatory's passport & Emirates ID
  • Correct commercial usage classification (office / retail / industrial / accommodation)

Additionally for renewals & cancellations

  • The existing Ejari certificate or its number
  • Renewed tenancy contract (renewals) or handover/NOC proof (cancellations)

7. Validity, Renewal & Cancellation

Validity

An Ejari certificate is valid for the term of the tenancy contract it records — typically one year. When the contract ends or renews, the registration must follow.

Renewal

Renewal is a fresh registration against the renewed contract. Let it lapse and everything chained to it starts failing — license renewal blocks are the classic symptom. Our renewal service includes free WhatsApp reminders before your expiry every year.

Cancellation

Because a unit can hold only one active Ejari at a time, the outgoing registration must be cancelled before a new tenant can register. Cancellation is also required in many license-amendment and premises-change scenarios. We handle cancellations cleanly so nothing blocks the next step.

8. The 7 Most Common Rejection Reasons

  • An active Ejari already exists on the unit (previous tenant never cancelled)
  • Usage type mismatch — e.g. registering a restaurant as a plain retail shop
  • DEWA premise number doesn't match the unit in DLD records
  • Name spelling differs between passport, contract and license
  • Missing or outdated landlord title deed
  • Contract areas or unit numbers inconsistent with the title deed
  • Expired trade license attached to a commercial registration

10. What Happens If You Don't Register

An unregistered tenancy leaves you outside the system: no trade license transactions, no DEWA in your name, no family sponsorship from that address, and no legal standing at the Rental Dispute Center if a conflict with your landlord ever arises. Non-registration can also expose the parties to penalties under Dubai's tenancy regulations. In short — the cheapest thing about Ejari is doing it properly, on time.

11. Glossary — Speak Fluent Ejari

Ejari
Arabic for “my rent” — Dubai's mandatory online tenancy registration system, launched under Law No. 26 of 2007 and operated by RERA under the Dubai Land Department.
RERA
Real Estate Regulatory Agency — the regulatory arm of the Dubai Land Department that governs the rental market, the Ejari system and the rental index.
DLD
Dubai Land Department — the government body overseeing all real estate registration in Dubai, including Ejari.
DET / DED
Dubai's Department of Economy & Tourism (formerly DED) — issues and renews mainland trade licenses, and requires Ejari for premises.
Tawtheeq
Abu Dhabi's equivalent tenancy registration system, required for licenses, ADDC and visas in Abu Dhabi and Al Ain.
Title deed
The ownership document of the property, issued by DLD — a copy is required from the landlord for Ejari registration.
DEWA premise number
The unique number identifying a unit in the Dubai Electricity & Water Authority system — Ejari and DEWA records must match.
Makani number
Dubai's 10-digit geographic address for every building entrance — often used to pinpoint premises in registrations.
Unified tenancy contract
Dubai's standardized tenancy contract format, designed to be Ejari-ready.
Dubai REST
The Dubai Land Department's official app where any Ejari certificate can be verified instantly by its number.
RDC
Rental Dispute Center — Dubai's judicial arm for landlord-tenant conflicts; a registered Ejari is required to file a case.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Ejari required for residential tenancies too, or only commercial?
Both. Every tenancy contract in mainland Dubai — residential apartments and villas as well as commercial offices, shops and warehouses — must be registered in Ejari under Dubai's tenancy law. Residential Ejari is what unlocks DEWA, family visa sponsorship and school admissions.
Who is responsible for registering Ejari — landlord or tenant?
Under the regulations the landlord (or their property manager) carries the registration obligation, but in day-to-day practice tenants very often complete it, especially for commercial premises, because the tenant needs the certificate for their license, DEWA and visas. Either party can register with the correct documents.
Can one property have two active Ejari registrations?
No. A unit can hold only one active Ejari at a time. This is one of the most common causes of rejection — the previous tenant's Ejari was never cancelled. We resolve exactly this situation routinely.
Does a free zone company need Ejari?
If the premises are inside certain free zones with their own lease-registration systems, the free zone's system applies instead. But the moment a free zone company takes mainland premises — an office, shop or warehouse outside the free zone — Ejari becomes mandatory for that unit.
What happens if I never register my tenancy in Ejari?
You become invisible to the system: no trade license issuance or renewal, no DEWA in your name, no family visa sponsorship from that address, and — critically — no ability to file a case at the Rental Dispute Center if a conflict arises. Non-registration can also expose the parties to penalties under Dubai's tenancy regulations.
Is the Ejari certificate the same as the tenancy contract?
No. The tenancy contract is the private agreement between landlord and tenant; the Ejari certificate is the government's official record of that contract, with its own unique number. Authorities rely on the Ejari certificate, not the raw contract.
