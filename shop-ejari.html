What is Shop Ejari?

Every retail shop, showroom, salon, restaurant, cafeteria and kiosk in Dubai must register its tenancy contract as a Shop Ejari before the DED trade license can be issued or renewed. The same certificate is your gateway to the signboard (shopfront) permit, the DEWA connection for the premises, and municipality approvals for fit-out and food/health permits where applicable.

Delays here are expensive — every day your shop isn't open is lost revenue. Our team registers shop Ejari within 10–20 minutes in most cases: we validate the tenancy contract, check the title deed and premise details, fix common errors that cause rejections (wrong usage type, mismatched areas, missing DEWA numbers) and deliver your certificate on WhatsApp.

Whether it's a boutique in Karama, a cafeteria in Deira, a salon in Al Barsha or a showroom on Sheikh Zayed Road — one WhatsApp message starts the process.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need shop Ejari before getting my trade license?
Yes. DED requires a valid commercial Ejari for the premises before issuing or renewing a retail trade license. It is also required for your signboard permit and DEWA.
My tenancy contract has an error — can you still register?
This is one of the most common causes of rejection. Send us the contract on WhatsApp first — we will review it free of charge and tell you exactly what to fix before submission.
Can you register Ejari for a restaurant or salon?
Yes. Food and beauty premises have specific usage-type requirements in the Ejari system, and we handle those correctly the first time.
