About Ejari

What Is Ejari and Who Needs It?

Ejari (Arabic for "my rent") is the online tenancy registration system run by Dubai's Real Estate Regulatory Agency (RERA). Every commercial and residential lease in Dubai must be registered through Ejari before it can be used for official purposes. Businesses need an Ejari certificate to issue or renew a trade license with the Department of Economy and Tourism (DET), connect DEWA electricity and water, sponsor employee and family visas, and open or maintain a corporate bank account. Landlords and tenants also rely on Ejari to formalize residential contracts, since an unregistered lease has no legal standing in a rental dispute before the Rental Dispute Settlement Centre.

Because Ejari requirements differ by activity, license type and emirate, we review your trade license and tenancy documents free of charge and match you with the correct — and most cost-effective — compliant option before you pay for anything.
