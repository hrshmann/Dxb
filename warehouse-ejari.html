What is Warehouse Ejari?

A Warehouse Ejari registers industrial and storage premises — warehouses, workshops, factories, cold stores and open yards — as required for trading, general trading, logistics, contracting and industrial licenses in Dubai. It is also mandatory for DEWA industrial connections and civil defence approvals.

Industrial Ejari has its own pitfalls: incorrect usage classification, mismatched plot and makani numbers, missing affection plans, and landlord documentation issues in older industrial areas. Our team deals with these daily across Al Quoz, Ras Al Khor, Dubai Industrial Park, DIP, Jebel Ali, Al Qusais Industrial and Umm Ramool — so your registration goes through the first time.

Send us your tenancy contract on WhatsApp for a free pre-check. If it's clean, your certificate is typically ready within 10–20 minutes; if not, we tell you exactly what to fix before you waste a single dirham.
