What is Ejari and why is it mandatory in Dubai?
Ejari (Arabic for 'my rent') is Dubai's official tenancy registration system operated under RERA. Every tenancy contract — residential or commercial — must be registered. Without a valid Ejari you cannot issue or renew a trade license, connect DEWA, process most visas, or complete many government transactions.
How fast can DXB Ejari deliver my certificate?
In most cases, within 10–20 minutes. Send complete documents on WhatsApp at +971 58 525 5484 and your approved, verifiable certificate typically lands before the chat goes quiet.
Which types of Ejari do you handle?
All of them: virtual Ejari, business center Ejari, separate office Ejari, shop/retail Ejari, warehouse/industrial Ejari, labor camp/staff accommodation Ejari — plus renewals and cancellations for every type.
Why don't you publish your prices?
Because they are genuinely the most competitive in the market, and publishing them would just help competitors match us. Every visitor gets today's exact price on WhatsApp within minutes — no obligation, no spam, no hidden charges.
Is your Ejari genuine and verifiable?
100%. Every certificate we issue goes through official RERA-approved channels and can be verified instantly in the Dubai REST app using the certificate number.
What documents do I need?
It varies by type, but typically: tenancy contract, landlord's title deed copy, tenant's Emirates ID or passport, and trade license for commercial registrations. Send what you have on WhatsApp — we pre-check everything free.
Do I need to visit any office?
Never. The entire process — documents, payment, certificate delivery — happens on WhatsApp. Clients register Ejari with us from abroad routinely.
Can you fix a rejected Ejari application?
Yes, and it is one of our specialties. Most rejections come from usage-type errors, mismatched premise numbers or landlord documentation issues — send us the rejection details and we will resolve it.
Do you serve areas outside Dubai?
Yes — all seven emirates. Tawtheeq in Abu Dhabi and Al Ain, municipality tenancy attestation in Sharjah, Ajman, RAK, Fujairah and UAQ, plus business setup and PRO services nationwide.
Can you handle my trade license renewal along with Ejari?
Yes — bundling both is our most popular package and saves you both time and money. One WhatsApp message covers everything.
What payment methods do you accept?

Do you also do travel visas?
Yes — through our parent company OK for Travel & Tourism (oktravel.ae): visit visas for all countries, Schengen/UK/US/Canada applications, Umrah packages, flights and hotels.
